## Hi, I'm Dileep  Cheerla 👋

Power systems engineer at **GridAxon, Inc.** and graduate researcher at **Texas Tech University**, working on **inverter-based resources (IBRs)**, **electromagnetic transient (EMT) modeling**, and the grid impact of **large AI / data-center loads**.

I use this page to share the data, figures and scripts behind my papers, so other people can check and reuse them.

### Research areas
- Large data-center loads: LVRT, load smoothing and co-located BESS support
- EMT–RMS model benchmarking and dynamic model validation for IBRs, PV and BESS
- Grid-forming and grid-following converter control: current limiting, virtual impedance, fault ride-through
- Reactive-power compliance planning for IBR plants
